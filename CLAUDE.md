# Za: instructions for Claude Code

Za is a Godot 4.7.2 pizza shop management game. The design is in [`GDD.md`](GDD.md): read the sections that touch your task before gameplay work, and the whole thing when you're new to the project.

## Rules

- **Use Godot best practices.** Write idiomatic Godot 4 code that follows the official docs and the GDScript style guide.
  - Use statically typed GDScript only (no C#).
  - Build from small, reusable scenes and compose them rather than building deep inheritance.
  - Follow "signals up, calls down" so child nodes don't reach into their parents.
  - Use custom `Resource` types for data (recipes, stations, tasks and so on) instead of hard-coding it.
  - Use autoloads sparingly.
- **Target Godot 4.7.2 (Forward+).** Use APIs that exist in that version; check unfamiliar ones with the `godot-api` skill. Don't guess.
- **Don't invent design specifics.** The GDD is intentionally high-level. If numbers, formulas, systems, assets or schedules aren't covered there, ask the designer instead of making them up.
- **Make systems work; don't chase balance.** Check for real breakage (errors, stalls, deadlocks), but don't tune or measure balance unless asked.
- **Use placeholder art and audio** unless the designer says otherwise.
- **Keep `GDD.md` current.** When the designer makes a design decision, update the relevant section there, keeping it short and high-level. Keep the map below current when files move or systems are added.
- **Communicate briefly.** Give short, plain-language summaries. Don't add unrequested extras or expand the scope.
- **Use agents as needed** for research, exploration and independent tasks. Don't spawn agents for small changes.

## Ask first

Check with the designer before:
- Making or changing any design decision.
- Adding a third-party plugin, addon or any other dependency. Stay with vanilla Godot unless the designer approves.
- Doing a large refactor or deleting files.

## Tools

- Godot (Steam install): `C:\Program Files\Steam\steamapps\common\Godot Engine\godot.windows.opt.tools.64.exe`. Running it headless is allowed, to check the project loads and to verify your work before reporting it done. After adding a new `class_name`, run it once with `--headless --import`.
- Git isn't on PATH. Use SourceTree's bundled copy: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Atlassian\SourceTree\git_local\cmd\git.exe`.
- **Tests:** `.\tests\run_tests.ps1 -Quiet [name]` runs `tests/test_*.gd` headless (the full suite takes about 20 s). Run it after gameplay changes, and add a test for each new system. See the `run-tests` skill. Tests run with every pawn on shift all day unless they set `keep_schedules`. `tests/playtest.gd` (not in the suite) plays the real starting shop for several days at top speed and prints each day's orders, misses by cause, money, stock and where staff time went: `godot --headless --path . --fixed-fps 60 --script res://tests/playtest.gd -- days=7`. Task and wait times are in shop-clock minutes, so changing the day's length doesn't need retuning.
- Aseprite (Steam install): `C:\Program Files\Steam\steamapps\common\Aseprite\Aseprite.exe`, usable with `-b` for exports.

## Workflow

- Commit locally only, and only when the designer asks; the designer pushes with SourceTree. Make small, focused commits with clear messages. Keep `.godot/` out of version control.
- Plan before building any large feature. Build the smallest playable slice first, then add systems one at a time.

## Project layout

- `scenes/`, `scripts/`, `resources/` (custom `Resource` data), `Assets/` (art, audio, fonts), `tests/`, `docs/` (sprite list, draw order), `art_templates/` (drawing guides, ignored by Godot). Organize by feature inside them.
- Files and folders in `snake_case`; classes and nodes in `PascalCase`.

## Where things live in `scripts/`

`world/world.gd` (the `world.tscn` root) only sets up and connects the systems, and handles input. Everything else lives in its own folder:

- **`work/`**: `WorkManager` hands out jobs to idle pawns by priority (order jobs, shop jobs and chores like washing; restocking only as filler), walks them through each job (stops, then the work spot), and sends idle pawns wandering. It also runs shifts (`update_shifts()`): pawns walk in the front door when their shift starts and out when it ends, and the manager (or the best Managing pawn on shift) quotes. It's the hub most gameplay goes through. `WorkSpot` is a station spot a job could use.
- **`tasks/`**: `Job` (one step of an order, worked by one pawn) and `JobStop` (a stop on the way: pick up, set out). `TaskType` is a kind of work (stretch, bake, cut) in a `WorkType` column of the priority grid.
  - `TaskBehavior` plans and carries out a task: what's fetched, where it's done, what happens to the items. A new kind of task gets a subclass in **`tasks/behaviors/`** set on its `TaskType` resource. Examples: `IngredientBehavior` (dough from a container), `AtContainerBehavior` and `IntoContainerBehavior` (work at or into a box), `StackBehavior`, `ThrowOutBehavior`, `RackWhenFullBehavior` (Load oven: park the pizza on a pizza rack while the ovens are full), and restocking (`FoldBehavior`, `PutAwayBehavior`, `PutAwayContainerBehavior`, `SetOutContainerBehavior`). Dishes: `WashBehavior` (a chore: one pawn takes a sink's dirty batch through its basins), `BringDishesBehavior` (to the sink), `StoreCleanDishBehavior` (sink to Clean Dishes storage) and `PutAwayDishBehavior` (Clean Dishes storage to its storage, filled on the way if the `Dish` has `filled_at`, e.g. a sauce bucket at the bulk sauce bucket), for any dish in the `DishCatalog`, `ReturnBucketBehavior` (a sauce bucket left on the floor). `FloorCleanBehavior` (a chore worked standing on a floor cell via `WorkManager.start_floor_job()`, nearest first) with `MopBehavior` (dirt) and `SpillBehavior` (spills, left by steps with a `TaskType.spill_chance` like sauce), `WipeBehavior` (a chore: wipe down the nearest dirty station; `WorkManager` dirties a station as work finishes there, unless the `TaskType` has `dirties_station` off). `HaulCaseBehavior` puts a delivered supply case away from the delivery pallet (units into storage, or poured into a bulk tub). `PortionBehavior` (sauce) swaps an empty bucket for the prep table's spare first. `PutAwayDishBehavior` fills at a bulk tub (sauce bucket, dough container) using up its level. `PanBehavior` (cheese) takes portions from a station's `IngredientPan` (in `stations/`), and `RefillPanBehavior` refills a low one from a bag in storage, then throws the bag out.
- **`pawns/`**: `Pawn` walks the paths and works the jobs it's given, and animates. Everything it draws sits under its `Body` node, which moves (and sorts) when it steps aside for another pawn, never toward a wall or furniture. It doesn't decide anything; `WorkManager` does. It holds its priorities, skill levels, whether it's the manager and its `WorkSchedule` (48 half hours, Work or Off; away while off); `SkillRules` sets how levels scale work time and grow with practice. `PayRules` sets its fair wage (from its best skill, more for the manager), how its wage against that moves mood, and when it asks for a raise (checked as each day starts by `world.gd`). `PawnMood` is its mood, stress, energy (one break a day, its length by scheduled hours: `WorkManager` sends a tired pawn to a table with the Break role via `BreakBehavior`, or they rest standing) and timed `MoodEffect`s (mood bands change work speed and the skill a step counts for in quality; very low mood brings a breakdown with no new jobs), set by `MoodRules`. `PawnTrait`s (rolled by `TraitRules`, never two from one group) multiply stress gain, work speed, slacking and social need. `Social` (a node in `world.tscn`, set by `SocialRules`) gives each pair a chemistry; pawns near each other change their opinions by it and fill their social need, which moves mood. `WorkManager` decides when the shop is rushing (too many tickets per pawn, or one past its quote) and tells the pawns.
- **`stations/`**: `Station` is a placed piece of equipment (spots that hold items, a dirt level that tints its art, plus supply stock for storage, from its role or its data's built-in storage, like the prep table's hidden sauce cooler). Its type is a `StationData` resource (art, footprint, facings, `StationSpot`s, `StorageLayout`s). Surfaces are split into slots (`SlotGrid`, 8×8 per cell); each spot's item sits in its work area of slots, side items (`SideItem`, e.g. the sauce bucket) sit in the station's side areas, and items draw back to front. `StationRole` is a role a general table or rack can take (Prep, Boxing, storage of a `StorageKind`); a role can bring its own side items (Boxing's pizza cutter). A `DishSink` on the data makes it a dish sink; its `DishBoards` node holds the dirty and clean drainboard piles. A `BulkTub` on the data makes it a bulk tub (bulk sauce bucket, bulk dough tub) with a portion level that fills dishes and takes deliveries. A `DeliveryDock` makes it a delivery drop-off: its `DockCases` node holds and draws the cases. Storage starts empty; `Station.stock_up()` fills the starting shop's (layouts marked `stocked_at_start`). A `WalkIn` on the data makes it a walk-in cooler: its `WalkInRoom` node draws the floor and walls. Its walls stand on cell edges (`ShopFloor.set_edge_blocked()`), so its floor is open for racks the player builds inside; `BuildMode` carries them along when it moves. A role with `needs_walk_in` (Dough storage) is only offered inside one (`Station.get_available_roles()`).
- **`items/`**: `Item` is anything 1×1 that sits on a station, the floor, a pawn's hands or another item (pizza in a box). `IngredientContainer` is a multi-unit tub (dough); `Dish` is something washed at a sink (an empty dough tub or sauce bucket; `DishCatalog` lists them); `PortionContainer` is a `SideItem` a task takes portions from in place (the sauce bucket, used by `PortionBehavior`); `RotatedArt` pairs art with its quarter-turned version. `DonenessRules` sets how done a pizza comes out of the oven (set on the bake `TaskType`; `WorkManager` times the take-out by Oven skill).
- **`tickets/`**: `TicketRack` holds `Ticket`s in Wait and Make (pawns only work Make, top to bottom). A ticket's `OrderItem`s (one per pizza) are worked in parallel, one job at a time, then stacked and handed off.
- **`orders/`**: `OrderSource` places random orders, more in rushes (none without a manager); each one sends a customer in. The manager quotes each order's wait: `WaitEstimate` is the queue-aware guess (with `WorkManager`'s recent average walk per job) and `QuoteRules` sets how far off the manager is by Managing skill (`WorkManager.quote_order()`).
- **`customers/`**: `Lobby` moves the visible customers: in the front door, the register line (it holds a customer's take-order `Job` on hold until they reach the register, and turns arrivals away when full), a spot in the lobby while they wait, the pickup counter, and back out. `Customer` only walks and carries its order (pawn art with its shirt recolored by `resources/customers/shirt_swap.gdshader`).
- **`reviews/`**: `ShopReputation` keeps the shop's rating (newer reviews count more), its popularity (word of mouth, grown or shrunk by each review's stars) and today's reviews for the day report. `ReviewRules` scores each customer's `Review` (stars, complaints, compliments) and sets how the rating and popularity scale `OrderSource`'s demand.
- **`menu/`**: `Recipe` is a menu item: its steps (task types) and price. `QualityRules` scores a finished item (ingredients, the skill of its hands-on steps (`TaskType.affects_quality`), doneness, freshness) into a tier; `WorkManager` sets it when the item's last step is done, and reviews and the rating use the order's worst tier.
- **`economy/`**: `ShopFinances` (cash and today's tally), `Bill` (daily running costs), `DayReport` (the end-of-day summary).
- **`supply/`**: `SupplyItem` (something bought by delivery: case size, where it's kept, price) listed in a `SupplyCatalog`. `Deliveries` keeps each supply's target and, on delivery days at opening, tops it up in whole cases onto the delivery pallet; `world.gd` pays for it.
- **`world/`**: `PixelPick` (finds the front-most opaque sprite at a click, in Godot's draw order; a station's strips point it into the station's canvas), `ShopFloor` (floor tiles and the pathfinding grid; furniture blocks cells, walk-in and inner walls block edges; lobby tiles are off limits to wandering), `FloorDirt` (each cell's dirt, added by pawn and customer steps via their `cell_reached` signals, and spills; drawn between the floor and the entities), `BuildMode` (place, move and sell stations), `ShopClock` (day, weekday, time, game speed; a day spans the first shift or prep to the last shift or closing, set by `world.gd`) with `ShopHours` (prep, open, last orders, close), `ShopCamera`, `ShopWalls` with `WallStyle` and `WallPiece` (outer walls, doors and inner walls such as the kitchen/lobby wall; its edge-wall builder is shared with walk-ins). The starting shop's layout (floor in `shop_floor.tscn`, walls and stations in `world.tscn`) is described in `GDD.md`.
- **`ui/`**: `Hud` owns the panels and passes the player's requests up: `ClockPanel` (top left), `PawnBar` (`PawnCard`s along the top: portrait, mood bar, status; click selects, double-click centers the camera), `TicketRackPanel` (`TicketColumn`, `TicketCard`), `BottomBar` (opens `PriorityPanel`, `SchedulePanel` (a drawn `ScheduleGrid`: shifts painted per pawn, and the Hours bar's draggable markers), `BuildPanel`, `SupplyPanel` (stock, targets, next delivery) one at a time above it, and reopens the last `DayReportPanel`), and the inspect panels in the bottom-left corner: `StationPanel`, `PawnPanel` (tabs: Needs, Mood, Social, Skills, Pay), `ItemPanel` (the selected item's name and quality).

**How an order flows:** `OrderSource` → the manager quotes it → its `Customer` lines up in the `Lobby` → at the register a Cashier pawn takes it and `world.gd` prints a `Ticket` on the `TicketRack` → `WorkManager` gives each `OrderItem`'s next `Job` to a pawn, planned by its `TaskBehavior` → the `Pawn` walks its stops and works at a `Station` spot, moving `Item`s → finished items are stacked and handed off → the customer collects it at the pickup counter, `ShopFinances` takes the payment and the customer leaves a review with `ShopReputation`. Late tickets are cancelled (a 1-star review) and their leftovers thrown out.

Data lives in `resources/` by type (`stations/`, `tasks/`, `recipes/`, `items/`, `work_types/`, `station_roles/`, `storage_kinds/`, `storage_layouts/`, `orders/`, `reviews/`, `bills/`, `supplies/`, `pawns/` (with `schedules/`), `shop/` (shop hours), `walls/`, `tilesets/`, `ui/`). Scenes mirror the scripts in `scenes/` (`world/`, `stations/`, `pawns/`, `customers/`, `items/`, `ui/`).
